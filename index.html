Every club on your campus, in one place

Atlas is the app universities use to host their clubs. One home for the club directory, events, and membership — open only to people at your university.

About

Campus clubs run on scattered group chats, paper sign-up sheets, and posters nobody reads twice. Atlas replaces that with a single place students actually check: what clubs exist, what is happening this week, and how to join.

Atlas is strictly for universities. Every account belongs to a verified student, staff member, or recognised society at a participating institution, so a club here is a real campus club, not a public group.

University only. Sign-up requires a verified university account. Communities outside a university are not supported.

Features

  • Your campus club directory

    Browse every registered society at your university, with a short description and how to get involved.

  • Events and sign-ups

    Clubs post what is coming up, students RSVP in a tap, and organisers see who is coming.

  • Membership that runs itself

    Join, leave, and track who is a member, without chasing anyone for a spreadsheet.

  • One university per space

    Spaces are scoped to a single institution, so students see their own campus and nobody else's.
