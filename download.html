Mobile and desktop. Around 40 MB. No account needed to browse this site.

How to get started

  1. Download the app using the button above.
  2. Sign in with the university email address issued by your institution.
  3. Confirm the verification email, then pick your campus.
  4. Browse the club directory and join the societies you are interested in.

Who can use Atlas

Atlas is strictly university based. Accounts are limited to verified students, staff, and recognised societies at participating universities.

No university email? You cannot create an account yet. Ask your students' union or club committee whether your institution has joined Atlas.
